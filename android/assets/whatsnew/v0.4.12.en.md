This release finishes the motion work: everything that slid in now slides back out, the send animation is gone, and streaming text is smoother.

## Improved

- **Motion goes both ways**:
  - Closing the sidebar slides the panel back from wherever it is, and only the backdrop colour fades — no more leftover ghosting. Tapping the backdrop, pressing Back, pushing it away or letting go halfway all close it the same way. Tap to open again mid-close and it springs back from where it stopped.
  - Settings sub-pages and project / scheduled task details: on phones, going back slides the page out to the right, and going in pushes the list back a little and dims it. On tablets, switching categories fades the old content out.
  - Switching between pages fades out the old one while the new one floats up.
- **Smoother streaming**: providers often send a big chunk of text at once, which used to make the reply lurch. Text is now released at an even pace and speeds up when there's a backlog, catching up in about a quarter of a second.
- **Send animation removed**: the flight from the send button to the bubble often didn't line up, so it's gone; your message simply appears in its bubble.

## Fixed

- **The greeting on a new chat rendered twice**: it floated up, then faded in again.
- **DeepSeek balance showed ¥0.00**: when the money was in the USD account, only the empty CNY entry was read, and refreshing could flip it back to 0. The entry with a balance is now used, any other currency with money is listed in the details, and today's spend works again.
