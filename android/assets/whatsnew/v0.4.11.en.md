This release is all about motion: the sidebar, menus, pages and panels now show where they come from and where they go, instead of popping in and out.

## New

- **Interface motion**, built on spring physics: things settle in with a slight bounce and leave crisply.
  - The sidebar slides in with its items following one after another — also when you swipe it open, settling with a spring when you let go.
  - Menus, dropdowns, the quota card, the model panel and the permission mode menu grow out of the button you tapped; dialogs fade in their backdrop and float their card up.
  - Pages, Settings, settings sub-pages, and project / scheduled task details push in from the right on phones and slide back when you go back; on tablets they float up. Files, viewer, git and terminal slide in from the right when docked.
  - When a tool finishes, "Done" pops and the card flashes a green ring; the progress hint in a reply rolls up to its new text.
  - Jumping to a chapter bumps its title and sweeps a blue line across; picking a line in Lineage dims the rest and pops its card up.
  - When you send, your message flies from the send button along an arc into its bubble.
  - Only position and opacity animate; streaming text, chat scrolling and long lists are left alone, so older phones stay smooth.
- **Motion setting**: Settings → General → Interface motion: System / On / Off. "System" turns it off when Reduce motion is on.

## Improved

- **Opening Updates checks right away**: every visit to Settings → Update checks again, no need to tap "Check for updates". It skips the check while something is downloading or installing, or if it just checked.
- **The "System" UI font covers more**: CJK text on tool cards and next to numbers, and the greeting on a new chat, now use the system font too. Claude's reply text follows the "Response font" setting — pick "Sans" to have it follow the system as well.

## Fixed

- **"Customize pinned items" couldn't scroll**: with many pinned items or "More" expanded, the bottom of the sidebar was out of reach on phones. Navigation and recent chats now scroll together; nothing changes when everything fits (#24, thanks @BaiXiaoTao520).
- **Work Island stayed dark on Android 16**: on ColorOS 16 the installed app wasn't detected; it is now (thanks @STUTTEFRFIX, #25).
