Fixes v0.7.19 regressions including false Shizuku unauthorized status, silent self-update stalls, and cascading Settings HTTP 502 errors, alongside 256KiB transcript boundary paging, targetSdk 36 `$SHELL` trampoline propagation, and transient KeyStore error handling.

## Hotfixes & Stability Hardening

- **Shizuku process status & wireless keepalive**: Added fallback exit/liveness detection on `ShizukuRemoteProcess` when hidden Binder fields are blocked, eliminating false unauthorized states and hung privileged calls; bounded wireless ADB keepalive probes with deferred teardown handoff so turning keepalive off reliably reverts TCP 5555 and ADB state.
- **Silent in-app self-update**: Fixed foreground gateway pause race before installation and reworked `pipeInstall` with isolated temp paths, a startup sentinel, and bounded `pm install` timeouts with clean fallback to the system installer.
- **Settings HTTP 502 self-healing & KeyStore resilience**: Stopped stacked background polling on Device & Permissions, added in-place retry on transient gateway `502` responses, separated transient boot-time KeyStore exceptions from permanent signature corruption in `PageConsent`, and localized the Shizuku auto-start consent label across all 9 languages.
- **Transcript paging, binary seal & `$SHELL` trampoline**: Fixed `readSessionPage()` dropping adjacent JSONL records when a 256KiB chunk boundary lands right after a newline; deferred `strictMaps` verification until `stdio[3]` delivers the real `claude` PID under loader supervision; preserved the loader `$SHELL` trampoline across nested Go/shell invocations under targetSdk 36.
