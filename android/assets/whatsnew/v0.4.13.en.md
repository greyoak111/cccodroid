This release fixes motion in landscape and tones down the bounce.

## Improved

- **Less bounce**: the overshoot when menus and panels pop open is now 0.4× what it was; the sidebar and pages simply settle. Timing is unchanged.
- **Settings no longer jiggles in landscape**: switching categories crossfades the content in place, and Settings opens with a gentle zoom and fade. Switching between pages (say, Projects to Scheduled tasks) works the same way.

## Fixed

- **Closing the docked sidebar flashed**: in landscape the sidebar briefly turned back into a full-screen drawer, flashing its backdrop and blur. The panel now slides straight out while the content fills the space, and slides back in when you open it.
- **The sidebar glitched when toggled with the menu button in landscape**: it changed width and corners halfway through the animation; fixed.
- **Tapping the same section button again on the Extensions page twitched**: when you were already at that section, the page nudged a few pixels back and forth; it now stays put.
