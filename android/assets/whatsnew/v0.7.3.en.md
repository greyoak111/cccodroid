This release rebuilds the Shizuku one-tap activation: on this device (Honor Magic V6, Android 17) activation used to hang forever; delivery is now reliable and pairing is no longer misjudged. The activation flow has one **visible behavior change**, noted first.

## Behavior change: the app bounces to Home and back during activation

- After you tap Activate, the app briefly switches to the Home screen and returns on its own after about 3 seconds, then activation completes as usual. This is deliberate: Shizuku 13.5+ delivers its binder only when an app's process state changes, and the bounce triggers that delivery — so activation no longer stalls waiting.
- The credential wait now has two stages: first up to 60 seconds; if the binder still hasn't arrived, it keeps waiting and explains how to finish manually (up to 10 minutes total), instead of waiting blindly until a timeout error as before.

## Pairing is no longer misjudged as invalid

- After stopping the Shizuku service, re-activation sometimes demanded the pairing code again — actually wireless debugging had just restarted and its port wasn't ready yet, which was mistaken for an invalid key. Now only a genuine authentication rejection asks you to re-pair; on connection failure the app probes the real port and retries automatically, so a changed port heals itself.

## Anti-freeze tip

- New tip in the Shizuku section of Settings: Honor / Huawei and similar systems freeze Shizuku in the background; if activation is slow or fails, open system App management → Shizuku and enable "Ignore battery optimization".

## Notes

- Tested on device: first-time pairing activation and re-activation after stopping the Shizuku service both succeed on the first attempt.
- Upgrading does not touch existing sessions, settings or archives.
- Thanks to the user who reported the issue and verified every step.
