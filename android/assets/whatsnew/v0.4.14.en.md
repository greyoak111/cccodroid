This release brings developer tools to HTML previews and the in-app browser, and lets you pick a piece of a page and hand it straight to Claude.

## New

- **DevTools**: previewing an HTML file in the viewer now has a "DevTools" toggle, and the browser pane toolbar has "</>". Turn it on and Eruda's gear appears in the corner: elements (tap to select), console, network, storage and resources. Eruda ships with the app and works offline; only what's displayed is changed, never the file on disk. It stays on when you follow links inside the preview.
- **Pick an element for Claude**: with DevTools on, tap "Pick element" (⌖ in the browser pane), drag over the page to see what's outlined, and tap. Its selector and a short HTML snippet go into the message box, so you just write what to change. It's only text in the box; sending is up to you.

## Improved

- **Collapsing a step group collapses what's inside**: closing "N steps" also closes the steps, subagent runs and thinking you had open inside it.

## Fixed

- **The background suddenly blurred when you let go of the swiped-open sidebar**: the blur was off while dragging and snapped on at release, then snapped off when closing. It now fades in with your finger and fades out with the backdrop.
