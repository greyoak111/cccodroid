This release brings the first foldable fixes made on a real device: the strip behind the gesture bar, and the layout when an unfolded device is turned sideways.

## Fixes

- The strip behind the gesture bar at the bottom of the screen is no longer left empty. It used to show only the navigation bar color, which didn't match the sidebar or the input box, so on a foldable's inner and outer screens the interface looked cut short. The page now extends to the very bottom of the screen, and the sidebar's account row and the input box keep clear of the gesture bar on their own; the input box still moves up above the on-screen keyboard. This applies to phones, tablets and three-button navigation too (Android 11 and later) (#19).
- On an unfolded foldable turned sideways, the sidebar no longer turns into a drawer over the chat. Once the hinge became horizontal it no longer split the layout, and with a width of only about 880 the screen was treated as mid-size. Near-square large screens are now laid out like tablets, with the sidebar docked on the left; when a pane such as Files or Terminal is open and three columns don't fit, the sidebar steps aside (#19).
