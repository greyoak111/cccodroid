This release fixes a series of cases where model capacity and the compaction threshold didn't follow your changes, plus two small issues that left people stuck.

## Models and capacity

- After changing the context window or default model of the OpenAI bridge or a custom provider, the compaction slider in Settings, the top-bar scale and the chat's model label update right away, with no need to switch providers back and forth.
- When a project has its own provider, `/model` and `/autocompact` in its chats use that provider, and changing only the model in a chat no longer quietly moves it back to the global provider.
- The top bar of a project chat is no longer overwritten by the global model's window, and the Settings slider is drawn from the global model only, never borrowed from the current chat.
- Switching models quickly several times no longer lets a late, older response revert your choice and its capacity.
- A model that isn't in the provider's list now shows its capacity as unknown instead of borrowing another model's window, and a model-list request sent before you edited a provider can no longer write the old capacity back.

## Other

- A reply you interrupted while it was still running tools, before it said anything, now has a Delete button too.
- After uninstalling and reinstalling the OpenAI bridge in the app, the config page no longer keeps saying the existing bridge can't be migrated. Enter the key again and save; the old folder is renamed and kept as a backup. (Requires component r12.)
