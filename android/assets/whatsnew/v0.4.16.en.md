Phones in landscape are no longer treated as a squashed portrait screen: this release brings them as close to the tablet landscape layout as possible, and fixes a few problems that also showed up in portrait.

## Phone landscape

- **Docked sidebar and side panes**: the sidebar stays on the left, as on a tablet. Wide enough phones get three columns side by side; on narrower ones, opening a pane such as files or the terminal makes the sidebar step aside until the pane is closed.
- **The quick-switch row is back**: model, effort and permission under the input box used to be hidden in landscape. They're shown again.
- **Status bar hidden**: landscape gives the status bar's space back to the app. Swipe down from the top edge to bring it up for a moment.
- **Tighter spacing**: padding in the top bar, sidebar, input area and settings is trimmed, so more fits on a short screen. Nothing is taken away.
- **Full-screen settings**: settings used to shrink into a thin card on short screens. They now fill the screen, with categories on the left and content on the right.
- **Lineage shows the full metro map**: landscape used to fall back to the indented tree. It now gets the same map as a tablet, with panning and zooming.

## Fixed

- **After the first message of a new chat, the input box stayed two lines tall and pushed the send button out of place**: this happened in portrait too. The input height is now recalculated when the welcome screen goes away and on rotation.
- **The greeting stayed in the chat after the first message**: it carried Claude's name and looked like a reply. It now goes away once you send the first message.
- **With tall content, the top of the welcome screen got cut off**: the setup cards shown before a provider is configured, for example, pushed their heading off screen with no way to scroll back. Everything can be scrolled to now.
- **The model picker showed no model list on short screens**: the whole card now scrolls as one, and the model in use is listed first, so it's visible as soon as the picker opens.
- **Permission and long-press menus ran off short screens**: when they don't fit, they're capped in height and scroll on their own.
- **Dragging the lineage map all the way left left a blank strip on the right of the timeline**: the timeline background now spans the whole map.
- **A stray vertical line through the rounded corner where the docked sidebar meets the chat on wide screens**: the seam is clean now.
