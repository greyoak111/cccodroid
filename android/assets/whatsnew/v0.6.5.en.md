This is a patch release for 0.6.4 that fixes filing sessions in Lineage.

## Fixes

- In 0.6.3 and 0.6.4, filing and back-filling sessions (Lineage) from Claude's terminal always failed, saying the gateway "could not prove itself". 0.6.3 hardened protection against impersonation, which changed how the gateway proves its identity, and the terminal filing command broke as a result. Terminal tools now have their own way to verify the gateway, so filing and back-filling work again, and the impersonation protection stays in place.
- The "status reconcile" check in the toolchain self-test failed for the same reason and is fixed too.
