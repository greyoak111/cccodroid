This release is about Lineage: once you have lots of sessions, one map shows what branched from what, what each chapter did, and which session that old piece of work lives in.

## New

- **Lineage page.** A new Lineage entry in the sidebar draws all your sessions as a subway map. Time runs left to right, each line is a session, grouped into bands by project. Forks, rewinds, merges and handoffs each have their own look, and scheduled tasks appear as a dotted line. Drag with one finger, pinch to zoom time, double-tap to return to now. Tap a line to light up its family; a card at the bottom right shows where it came from and each chapter. In portrait on a phone it becomes an indented tree that just scrolls.
- **Session archiving.** Each time a session is compacted, a chapter ends and the AI in that session titles it; the session list shows the latest chapter's title. Every chapter gets an archive number like "a1b2·3" (first four characters of the session ID · chapter) that you can use to refer to it.
- **Tap a station to jump to that chapter.** On a tablet, tap a station on the map, or a chapter in the card or the session's Lineage menu, to jump to where that chapter starts. Compaction points show as a "Chapter n · title" divider. After a jump only the nearby pages are loaded and more are read as you scroll, so even sessions hundreds of MB long stay smooth. Tap the arrow at the bottom right to go back to the latest.
- **Older sessions work too.** Sessions from before this release have no archive, but their relationships are still worked out from the transcripts. Untitled chapters show their first prompt in grey, and older "go read that session and take over" handoffs are recognized. One-shot Q&As and misc sessions are hidden by default.
- **Backfill titles.** The Backfill button on the Lineage toolbar uses your global default model to title old chapters from the compaction summary at the end of each chapter, without rereading the whole transcript. Before starting it tells you the model, the number of chapters and roughly how many tokens it will use, and you can stop it at any time.
- **Tour.** "How to read" on the toolbar opens a hands-on tour with sample sessions: drag, zoom, tap a line, read chapters, tell the kinds of lines apart. There is a landscape and a portrait version.

## Fixes

- **Approval prompts with "Allow all" on.** With Allow all enabled, subagents still asked for permission before using tools. Claude Code is now started so it can switch to Allow all, and after switching, subagent tool calls are allowed too (except things that need your answer anyway, such as questions to you or leaving plan mode). Thanks to the community member who reported it.
- **Confusing "unsupported protocol" when testing an endpoint.** Some models are only offered over the OpenAI protocol by their provider, and the test returned a cryptic error. It now says plainly that the model doesn't support the Anthropic protocol, so pick another model or route (#23).
- **The "Writing parameters" count stopped moving.** With a subscription (the official API), "Writing Bash parameters · N chars" at the end of a reply often stayed stuck on the first few characters. The official API sends parameters in large chunks, and the last chunk landed inside the throttle window and was dropped. The final count is now reported when the parameters are done, and the elapsed time is shown too, so you can tell it isn't stuck even when the count holds still.
- Work Island: the title follows the current action and shows checklist progress (thanks @STUTTEFRFIX, #22).
